# AREA 51: THE SILENT RUNWAY

## Screenplay and shot plan
Each shot is 8 seconds. Narration and dialogue must fit the assigned time with pauses; do not speed-read.

### Scene 1: Arrival — 00:00–00:32
1. 00:00–00:08. Low tracking camera follows a dusty 1950s-style government sedan down an isolated Nevada desert road at dusk. Wheels turn, dust trails, scrub bends in the wind. Mountains surround a distant dry lake. Narrator: "They sent Mara into the desert to test an aircraft. Nobody mentioned the signal."
2. 00:08–00:16. Inside the moving car, Mara turns a sealed envelope over in her hands. She watches a distant fence approach through the windshield. Narrator: "The envelope contained a destination, a clearance number, and one instruction: ask nothing."
3. 00:16–00:24. At a fictional checkpoint a guard examines the envelope and raises the barrier. Mara drives onward. Dialogue, guard: "No cameras beyond this point." Mara: "I didn't bring one."
4. 00:24–00:32. Wide moving shot reveals runway lights across an immense dry lake; sedan approaches low hangars. Title added in editing: AREA 51: THE SILENT RUNWAY. Smaller line: An original fictional story inspired by the supplied PDFs. Narrator: "Behind the mountains, a hidden world was already awake."

### Scene 2: The test — 00:32–01:04
5. 00:32–00:40. In a hangar, engineers uncover a long, slender, fictional reconnaissance aircraft. Mara walks beneath its wing, looking up. Narrator: "There were no little green men waiting for her. There were engineers, exhausted pilots, and machines built to outrun danger."
6. 00:40–00:48. Elias hands Mara a battered stopwatch beside the aircraft. Dialogue, Elias: "When the instruments disagree, trust what you can measure." Mara: "And when the measurements disagree?"
7. 00:48–00:56. Cockpit close-up: Elias fastens his harness; instruments glow; ground crew pulls chocks. Dialogue, Elias: "Then measure again." Engines rise beneath the final words.
8. 00:56–01:04. The reconnaissance aircraft accelerates down the dry-lake runway and lifts into amber sky; dust and heat shimmer move naturally. Narrator: "For a moment, everything behaved exactly as it should."

### Scene 3: The signal — 01:04–01:36
9. 01:04–01:12. Mara watches an analog monitoring console. A second needle begins oscillating while a paper recorder feeds beneath it. Narrator: "Then a second signal appeared. Not interference. Not noise. A pattern."
10. 01:12–01:20. Close-up of Mara's hands comparing recorder traces with her stopwatch; she glances sharply toward the radio. Dialogue, Mara: "Elias, repeat your last heading." Elias over radio: "I haven't transmitted."
11. 01:20–01:28. An operator reaches to switch off the second receiver; Mara gently stops his hand. Dialogue, operator: "We were told to ignore that channel." Mara: "By someone who knew it was there?"
12. 01:28–01:36. Instrument-light reflection in Mara's eyes, intercut with the aircraft banking above dark mountains. Narrator: "The signal followed the aircraft. Every turn. Every change in altitude. Always a few seconds ahead."

### Scene 4: The choice — 01:36–02:08
13. 01:36–01:44. Elias steadies the controls; his expression changes as he hears Mara through his headset. Dialogue, Elias: "You see it too?" Mara: "How long has this been happening?"
14. 01:44–01:52. Mara walks rapidly down a fluorescent corridor with the paper trace in her hand. A locked file-room door stops her. Narrator: "The locked doors did not frighten her. The silence around them did."
15. 01:52–02:00. Mara returns to the console and adjusts a receiver dial while the stopwatch ticks. Dialogue, Mara: "Cut your transmitter for eight seconds. Let's see what follows."
16. 02:00–02:08. Cockpit close-up as Elias hesitates, then switches off the transmitter. Ground receiver needle continues moving. Narrator: "The aircraft went silent. The other signal kept speaking."

### Scene 5: Beyond the ridge — 02:08–02:40
17. 02:08–02:16. A desk lamp illuminates a hand-drawn terrain diagram as Mara marks two intersecting bearings with a pencil. Dialogue, Mara: "It's not coming from the aircraft. It's below the ridge."
18. 02:16–02:24. Mara and Elias, now back on the ground, drive an old utility vehicle along a rough service road under night sky. Narrator: "They followed it past the runway, past the last light, to a building missing from their briefing."
19. 02:24–02:32. In a small concrete equipment room, Elias opens a cabinet containing a humming unfamiliar electronic unit, copper coils and vacuum tubes. No aliens or bodies. Dialogue, Elias: "They said this room was empty." Mara: "Then somebody has been lying."
20. 02:32–02:40. Mara finds recorder strips already showing the exact sequence she measured, with her stopwatch resting beside them. Narrator: "Inside, she found the pattern again. Printed before the aircraft had taken off."

### Scene 6: An unanswered ending — 02:40–03:12
21. 02:40–02:48. The machine stops humming. Elias looks toward a closed inner door. Mara raises a hand for silence. Dialogue, Elias, quietly: "Tell me that's a fault." Mara: "I can't."
22. 02:48–02:56. A low pulse resumes; a narrow blue light appears beneath the inner door. Dust trembles on the cabinet. Narrator: "Perhaps it was a secret experiment. Perhaps the instrument was wrong. Neither explanation opened the door."
23. 02:56–03:04. Mara slowly reaches for the handle; Elias remains beside her. Close-up: the stopwatch starts moving backward. Dialogue, Mara: "This time, we write down everything."
24. 03:04–03:12. Cut before the door opens to vast desert at dawn; aircraft contrail crosses the pale sky. Fade to title and Fictional story / AI-generated cinematic imagery. Narrator: "Some mysteries begin with what we don't know. Others begin when we decide to look."
